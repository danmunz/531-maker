# 5/3/1 Routine - 2026-07-20 Home Gym Variant

## Summary

### Equipment Notes

- Built for a home setup with an Olympic bar, squat rack, adjustable bench, dumbbells up to 35 lb, and kettlebells up to 53 lb (one of each weight, so no double-kettlebell carries).
- All accessories are limited to barbell, dumbbell, kettlebell, bench, rack, or bodyweight work.
- No cable, machine, assisted pull-up, or two-handed carry movements are used in this version.
- Accessory sets/reps are unchanged from the standard program; pick the heaviest dumbbell or kettlebell (within the 35 lb / 53 lb caps) that lets you hit the prescribed rep range with 1-2 reps in reserve.
- Squat day's Superset B stays hinge-free (light single-leg/lower-body work only) since the main lift already loads the lower back. The barbell hinge work (Romanian Deadlift / Good Morning) lives on OHP day instead, which otherwise has no posterior-chain work at all.

### Sync Naming

- Folder Name: 5/3/1 - 2026-07-20 Block
- Routine Title Format: W[week]D[day]: [Main Lift Short]
- Short Lift Labels: Squat, Deadlift, Bench, OHP
